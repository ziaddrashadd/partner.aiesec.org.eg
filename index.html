How We Collaborate

Three ways your organization can work with us.

01 / Talent

Hire International Interns & Teachers

Bring international graduates and young professionals into your company or school across Egypt. Our team handles candidate shortlisting, visa paperwork, and local onboarding.

Global Talent (6–78 Weeks): International internships in business, tech, marketing, and engineering.
Global Teacher (6–78 Weeks): International educators and language instructors for schools and educational institutions.
02 / Employer Brand

Partner on Events & Youth Summits

Connect directly with ambitious youth aged 18–30 across Egypt. Position your company as an employer of choice by participating in our public forums, workshops, and national conferences.

Direct Youth Engagement: Deliver career-ready workshops, speak on panels, and run real case studies with attendees.
On-Ground Presence: Meet young talent face-to-face at our public exhibitions and leadership summits.
03 / Community Impact

Collaborate on CSR & Social Projects

Work with our local branches and international volunteers on community development, sustainability awareness, and educational initiatives aligned with the UN Sustainable Development Goals.

Global Volunteer (4–6 Weeks): Cross-cultural social impact projects partnering with local NGOs across Egypt.
Educational Initiatives: Youth awareness projects such as the World’s Largest Lesson and Youth4GlobalGoals.

Public Platforms

Flagship Public Events

Our largest public-facing gatherings that bring together companies, international guests, and thousands of young people across Egypt.

500+ Delegates • 30+ Companies • 20+ Speakers

Youth to Business (Y2B) Summit

Youth to Business connects business leaders and hiring teams directly with ambitious young people in Egypt. Hosted in Cairo, the summit bridges potential to real business opportunities through keynote talks, career-ready workshops, CV reviews, real case studies, and company networking spaces.

Focus Tracks: Corporate Careers, Entrepreneurship, Tech & AI, and Creators & Media.

Why partner: Meet talent face-to-face, run hands-on workshops, and position your employer brand.

Cultural Booths • Partner Keynotes • Live Shows

Global Village Festival

Global Village is our flagship cross-cultural festival where international exchange participants showcase their countries’ traditions, cuisines, and performances alongside thousands of Egyptian youth. Hosted at venues such as Nile University in Sheikh Zayed, it features country booths, speaker panels, partner keynotes, and cultural shows.

Who attends: Thousands of youth attendees, international exchange participants, embassies, and community partners.

Why partner: High-energy on-ground brand presence, booth activations, and cultural diversity alignment.

Annual Leadership Cycle

National Conferences

Throughout the year, we host four national conferences that bring together the active members and leaders running our 18 local branches. Partners join us to deliver training sessions, engage with our internal talent pool, and introduce their organizations.

Strategy & Leadership Induction

Momentum

Momentum kicks off our operational cycle. Newly appointed local branch leaders and members gather to align on annual goals and build functional skills in marketing, business development, finance, and operations.

Who Attends Local branch leaders, team leaders, and active members from across Egypt.
How Partners Participate Deliver workshops, speak to the plenary, and meet our active talent pool.

Institutional Traditions

Executive & Alumni Gatherings

AIESEC Annual Iftar

Held every Ramadan, our Annual Iftar brings together decades of AIESEC in Egypt alumni—now serving as corporate executives and founders—alongside our Board of Directors, partners, and current leadership team.

National Elections & Plenary

Our formal annual assembly where representatives from all 18 local branches convene with alumni and partners to elect the incoming Country President of AIESEC in Egypt.

International Scale

Global & Regional Summits Hosted in Egypt

Global Summit
