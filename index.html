Internal Talent Incubators

The National Leadership Cycle.

Four times a year, we gather the youth leaders running our 18 local branches for multi-day residential conferences. Partners join us to deliver workshops, run case competitions, and scout high-performing talent.

National Strategy & Leadership Induction

Momentum Conference

Momentum kicks off our operational year. Newly elected local branch leaders and members convene to align on national strategy, receive intensive functional training in marketing, business development, finance, and operations, and celebrate at our formal conference gala.

Target Audience Profile

Local branch presidents, department heads, and active members.

International Prestige

Global & Regional Summits Hosted in Egypt.

AIESEC in Egypt holds a proven track record of hosting major international congresses—including IC 2018, IC 2024, and MEAXLDS 2024—uniting delegations from over 100 territories in Egypt.

IC 2018 & IC 2024 Host Entity

AIESEC International Congress (IC)

The supreme global summit of the AIESEC network. Hosted in Egypt with country directors from 100+ nations, ministerial representation, and global corporate partners for a 10-day international leadership showcase.

Middle East & Africa Flagship

MEAXLDS Regional Leadership Seminar

The Middle East & Africa Exchange and Leadership Development Seminar hosted in Egypt, uniting regional youth directors and delegates to build cross-border exchange partnerships across the MEA region.
